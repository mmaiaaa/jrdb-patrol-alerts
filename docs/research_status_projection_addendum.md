# Research status addendum: full Cubberly development geometry audit

The five-camera Cubberly detector cache now covers 1,296 frames / 6,480 native images; this is only raw 2D detection. The development annotation-consistency check now covers all 1,296 frames and 4,516 matching near-person frame-camera 2D/3D pairs. The annotation-ego frame hypothesis gives 3,826 center-inside-box pairs versus 3,470 under annotation-upper. There are still 574 *evaluable* pairs whose ego-projected centers are outside their annotated native camera image, and unclipped disagreement cases await review. Source annotations, not independent image annotations or sensor-only localization, produce these counts. See `paper/cubberly_full_native_reprojection_note.md` and the paired review-queue summary for denominators and boundary exceptions.

**Research stage:** data access, basic multi-camera detection and calibration/annotation feasibility checks have advanced. The proposed online person-ranging, multi-view identity handling, track continuity, event policy and comparisons have not been validated. Timing verification, human review of encounter/negative intervals, independent validation groups, an untouched final-test run, and end-to-end robot latency remain critical gates. Do not convert the 1,296 repeated frames into an estimate of independent evidence or into a completion percentage. A methods/feasibility note is supportable now; reliability or event-performance claims require the remaining work.
