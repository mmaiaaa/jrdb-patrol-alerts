Writing workflow

`manuscript.md` is the canonical working draft. It can be read directly on GitHub and revised in the same commits as the research records. Convert to the selected journal's LaTeX or Word format once the venue and paper structure are chosen; changing formats now is not needed to begin writing.

Current status: motivation and an initial literature discussion are drafted; task/method/evaluation sections describe proposed work; results and conclusions explicitly remain pending. No author list, affiliation, funding statement, ethics statement, target journal, or experimental result has been invented.

Use past tense for performed experiments and future/proposed language for plans. After implementation, replace planned choices with the exact tested procedure, versions, settings, and exclusions. Update the evidence ledger with run IDs and output locations for every empirical claim.

`references.bib` contains verified starting bibliographic details, with an explicit note where final journal metadata needs checking. `related_work.csv` records what was actually inspected and what still needs full reading. Expand the closest-method comparison before claiming novelty.

Write the numerical abstract and conclusion after the final results and limitations are known. Keep null findings, failures, and unresolved limitations in the research record.
