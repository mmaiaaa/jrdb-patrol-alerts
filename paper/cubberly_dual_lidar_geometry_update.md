# Cubberly two-LiDAR reference audit (development draft)

The original JRDB [annotation procedure](https://arxiv.org/html/1910.11792v4) describes drawing 3D pedestrian boxes on a cloud merged from the **upper and lower Velodyne sensors**. The [sensor documentation](https://download.cs.stanford.edu/downloads/jrdb/Sensor_setup_JRDB.pdf) describes the two sensors' physical arrangement. These sources describe the original release and do not, by themselves, prove every processing detail or coordinate convention in the JRDB 2022 files used here.

The previous four-frame Cubberly audit decoded all selected upper clouds and found 114 valid pedestrian boxes; 13 had evaluable centers within the exploratory 3 m radius. Twelve of those 13 contained at least one raw upper-cloud point under each of two tested coordinate hypotheses. This is a **coverage count**, not detection recall or a decision that either coordinate convention is correct. In the first sample rows, reported `num_points` often exceeds upper-cloud box occupancy. Because the original annotation procedure merged two sensors, attributing such differences to a bad transform is premature.

`scripts/audit_jrdb_dev_dual_lidar_geometry.py` now counts measured points from **both** PCD streams inside each annotated 3D box, using `lower2upper` and `upper2ego` from the selected sequence's `lidars.yaml`. It reports counts separately for upper, transformed lower and combined points under each of the upper-frame and ego-frame label hypotheses. It records near evaluable cases missing point support for manual inspection. There is **no automatic choice** between coordinate hypotheses, inferred person range, association to a 2D detector box, or alert score. It imports the earlier tested 3D-box helper and compressed-PCD decoder from the repository.

Review the near cases, especially any with no combined support, against native image views, the cloud and the original annotation. Check whether boxes' local length and width axes and their `rot_z` sign match this diagnostic's stated geometric assumption; independently confirm the frame used for annotated centers. Reported `num_points` may apply preprocessing or different filtering and should not be treated as a perfect equality target. 3D annotation IDs and detector box indices are distinct. Labels can be consulted for *development evaluation only* and must not feed the online 2D-to-3D association or range algorithm.

After geometry and temporal alignment are verified, define a foreground-selection rule with an explicit unknown-range state. Evaluate distance errors on **independently matched** person examples and label-confirmed negatives, then evaluate encounter events. Reserve unopened sequences for the final held-out test.
