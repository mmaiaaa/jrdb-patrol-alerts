# Pilot dataset feasibility — manuscript working text

**Status:** Descriptive data audit; not an accuracy or significance result. Replace or integrate into `paper/manuscript.md` once the event protocol and independent data split have been finalized.

We first inspected the stationary indoor `bytes-cafe-2019-02-07_0` recording as a development pilot. The original JRDB study identifies this recording as stationary and documents the synchronized 360-degree RGB and LiDAR sensor setting ([Martín-Martín et al.](https://arxiv.org/html/1910.11792v4)). In the downloaded JRDB 2022 train archives, camera-0 images, both LiDAR streams, and 3D label filenames share 1,726 frame identifiers. We identified 39,567 labeled pedestrian-frame observations from 37 identities, including 14,518 evaluable observations within an **exploratory**, horizontal 3 m box-center radius. Grouping successive in-radius labels by identity yielded 37 candidate intervals involving 17 identities; these are descriptive intervals, not independently verified entries or ground-truth security events. Some persons were already within range at the beginning, and at least one remained within range in every pilot frame. This scene therefore offers no person-free frames under the provisional all-around 3 m rule and cannot alone establish background false-alert rates.

Stitched-image pose annotations show qualitative keypoint alignment with people across one visually inspected panorama, but they are incomplete as visibility ground truth. The stitched annotation rows report a 752-pixel image width whereas the native panorama measures 3,760 pixels; keypoint x-coordinates nevertheless extend across the wider image. The 3D `interpolated` flag is true for every within-radius pilot observation. Before deriving scored reference events, we will inspect the meaning and quality of these labels, verify robot-frame geometry and actual timing, and audit entries and negative intervals on moving recordings. No algorithmic performance or deployment claim follows from this pilot audit.
