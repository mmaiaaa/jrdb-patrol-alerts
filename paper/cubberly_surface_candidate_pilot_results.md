# Cubberly measured-surface pilot and projection review

**Development-only exploratory evidence.** Four Cubberly frames produced 142 raw detector boxes across five cameras. The measured upper+lower LiDAR procedure emitted **77 dominant-band surface candidates**, **39 single-band surface candidates**, **25 ambiguous abstentions**, and **one insufficient-support abstention**. Thus 116/142 boxes contain a candidate *surface radius* under these selected exploratory settings; this is **not coverage of real people or validated person-range accuracy**. Multiple separated depth bands inside a 2D box remain common. In the first logged camera-0 box at frame `000087`, the nearest supported band is around 4.04 m ego XY while a more populated band yields a 5.65 m surface candidate. Dominance of returns does not identify which surface is the person.

This descriptive review does not resolve provenance of each annotated ID, exact sensor timing or the meaning of 3D box-center XY in this release. The user's `human_review_template.csv` remains blank; do not silently fill it with these tentative notes. Reference 2D boxes were [initialized by 3D projection then manually refined](https://arxiv.org/html/1910.11792v4), so a projected 3D center falling in a reference 2D box is correlated annotation evidence.

The separate `scripts/evaluate_jrdb_dev_surface_candidates_offline.py` script joins **cached sensor-only candidate CSV** to native 2D and 3D development annotations after sensing has finished. It matches detections to evaluable native 2D boxes at IoU ≥0.5 with deterministic one-to-one maximum-cardinality matching, reports conditional discrepancies of measured surface radius against annotated 3D ego-center XY radius, signed bias, candidate abstentions, and 3 m proximity subset denominators. It never chooses a cluster from a label. An absolute difference between a surface and a geometric center is a **diagnostic discrepancy**, not validated person-distance error; sparse point clouds, multiple people, partial visibility, interpolation and reference-coordinate uncertainty still apply. Keep reference labels inaccessible to online sensing. Four selected frames and a single development recording cannot support a generalizable alert claim. Full development coverage, hand-checked matches, original timestamps, independent event adjudication and untouched moving-location test groups are later gates.
